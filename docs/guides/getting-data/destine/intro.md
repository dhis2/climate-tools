---
title: DestinE Earth Data Hub
---

The EU-funded [Destination Earth (DestinE)](https://platform.destine.eu/) initiative includes an [Earth Data Platform](https://earthdatahub.destine.eu/) for hosting and providing access to commonly used datasets optimized for efficient cloud-access. This includes a range of European-based climate datasets from the ECMWF, Copernicus, and others. The full list of available datasets can be found [on their catalogue page](https://earthdatahub.destine.eu/catalogue). 

The `dhis2eo.data.destine` module contains convenience functions for downloading some of the datasets available from the Earth Data Hub. See:

- [How to download ERA5 climate data](era5-download.ipynb)
